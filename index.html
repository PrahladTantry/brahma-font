All consonants

The shirorekha is drawn only over consonants joined by a virama: one bar runs across a whole conjunct and stops mid-letter on the last consonant, and a dead consonant carries a bar over its whole width. Vowel signs sit above the bar. Letters that are still being drawn (for example ष) may appear blank.
